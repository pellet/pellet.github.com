## Contact

Email [support@digimulti.com](mailto:support@digimulti.com). It helps to include:

- whether you use Videohog on a Mac or on Windows, and which version
- the address of the video, or the site it came from
- what happened, and any message Videohog showed.

## Downloading a video

Videohog downloads from a direct link to a video file, such as a link that ends in `.mp4`.

On a Mac:

1. Copy the link to the video file.
2. In Videohog, press **Paste URL**.
3. Press **Browse...** and choose the folder to save it in.
4. Press **Start**.
5. To watch while it downloads, choose a player such as VLC from the **Player** list and press **Play**.

On Windows:

1. Copy the link to the video file.
2. On the **Manual Download** tab, press **Paste**.
3. Choose the download folder.
4. Press **Start**.
5. Press **Watch now** to play it in Videohog, or **Watch in player** to open it in a player such as VLC.

## The speed upgrade

In Videohog Lite for Mac, Low uses 2 connections and Medium (the default) uses 3 throughout each transfer. High, Extreme and Advanced settings above 3 connections have a 20-second turbo period, then ramp down to 3 connections. The one-time speed upgrade, bought inside the app, lets those higher connection settings continue for the whole transfer.

If you bought the upgrade and Videohog does not show it, email [support@digimulti.com](mailto:support@digimulti.com) and we will sort it out.
