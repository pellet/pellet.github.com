Videohog

Faster video downloads. Start watching straight away.

Videohog downloads a video over several connections at once. You can open it in a player such as VLC while the rest of the file is still coming down.

What it does

  • Downloads each video over several connections at once, which can make large downloads faster.
  • Opens supported videos, such as MP4, in a player such as VLC before the download finishes.
  • Lets you choose how many connections to use and how large each section is.
  • On Windows, it also has its own player, a browser tab that finds the videos on a page, a live speed graph, and resumes partial downloads.
